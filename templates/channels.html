How email works

  • Staff email your helpdesk address. Each unread email becomes a request, and the reply goes back by email.
  • We know which company they belong to from their address in People, or from the company's email domain (set on the company page).
  • They reply FIXED to close it, HELP to get a technician, or anything else to add detail.
  • Requests made on the help link get an email when a technician replies.
  • The mailbox is checked once a minute.

How WhatsApp works

  • Staff message your WhatsApp business number. We match them by the number saved for them in People, so add numbers with the country code.
  • They reply 1 if it is fixed or 2 to get a technician.
  • WhatsApp only lets a business reply freely within 24 hours of the person's last message. A technician reply after that will show as not delivered.

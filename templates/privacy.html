Keeping clients apart

Every ticket, person, device and company guide is tagged with its company. A company's staff only ever see their own requests, and guides written for one company are never used to answer another.

Email and WhatsApp

If you switch these on, requests and replies on those channels pass through your mail provider and Meta, under their own terms. The mail and WhatsApp passwords are kept in a separate settings file, not in the database, and are never shown on screen.

Taking data out

Open a company and press Download all data to get its tickets, conversations, people, devices, guides and checklists as spreadsheet and text files. A client can leave with everything at any time.

Deleting data

The account owner can delete a company from its page. That removes all of its records from the database for good.

What the AI is allowed to do

  • It only suggests steps a staff member can safely do alone: restart, reconnect, check a setting.
  • It answers at most {{ max_replies }} times on a ticket. After that a technician takes over.
  • It never changes anything itself. It can only write a reply.
  • Its reply is checked before it is shown. Anything involving command lines, system settings, switching off security software or opening a device is blocked and sent to you instead.
  • You can switch it off for any single company.

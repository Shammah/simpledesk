Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Who we are

{{ app_name }} is a helpdesk run by {{ business }}. IT providers use it to look after the computers and accounts of the businesses they support. This policy covers the {{ app_name }} website and the {{ app_name }} app on Google Play, which shows the same website.

When an IT provider uses {{ app_name }} for their clients, that IT provider decides what information goes in and is responsible for it. We store and process it for them.

What we collect

  • Technician accounts: name, email address and a password. We store passwords in a scrambled form that cannot be read back.
  • People asking for help: name, work email address and, if their IT provider adds it, a WhatsApp number and job title.
  • Requests: what people write when asking for help, the replies, and when each request was opened and closed. Passwords and card-like numbers typed into a request are removed before it is saved.
  • Equipment: device names, serial numbers, warranty dates and who uses them, when an IT provider records them.
  • On your device: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, and your choice of light or dark mode. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we use it for

Only to run the helpdesk: to receive requests, answer them, show technicians who needs help and with what, and keep records the IT provider can look back on or download. We do not sell information, show adverts, or use it to build profiles of people.

Who else handles it

  • DigitalOcean hosts the servers where the information is stored.
  • The IT provider's email service, if they turn on requests by email.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), if the IT provider turns on WhatsApp. Messages then also follow WhatsApp's own terms.
  • Anthropic, only if the IT provider turns on AI-written answers. The text of a request and the relevant how-to guides are sent to write a suggested answer. Names, email addresses and device details are not sent.

We share information with anyone else only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Information is kept while the IT provider's account is open. When an IT provider deletes a client company, its requests, people, devices and guides are removed straight away. Backup copies are kept for {{ keep_days }} days and then deleted.

Your choices

  • IT providers can download all information about a client company at any time, and delete it.
  • If you asked for help through your employer's IT provider, contact them first: they control your information. You can also contact us and we will pass your request on or help directly.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it.

Keeping it safe

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Each IT provider's information is kept separate from every other's. Access to the servers is limited to the people who maintain {{ app_name }}.

Children

{{ app_name }} is a tool for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
